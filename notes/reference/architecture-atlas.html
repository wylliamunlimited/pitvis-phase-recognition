Orientation

One decode, one encoder, two heads

The current best system is arst-v2:best@dinov2_ft for steps and instruments-v2:best@dinov2_ft for instruments. Both read the same cache, produced by the same fine-tuned DINOv2 encoder. Everything else in this atlas is either an earlier encoder, an earlier head, or the floor.

Encoder A

ResNet-50 — the original cache

Two feature spaces are built from this network: resnet50 (ImageNet weights, frozen) and resnet50_ft (5 epochs on PitVis frames). Both emit 2,048-d, which is why they are interchangeable at load time — and why pitvis-eval can silently score one against the other.

Figure 1 ResNet-50 — box height ∝ spatial resolution 23,508,032 params · 224² → 2048-d

Encoder B — the one that ships

DINOv2 ViT-B/14 at 224 px

Self-supervised, no classification head ever trained on it. Built at img_size=224 rather than the checkpoint's native 518, which is why build_model has to override resolve_data_config — the transform must describe the tensor the backbone actually sees. dinov2_ft is this network after 2 epochs on PitVis frames.

Figure 2 DINOv2 ViT-B/14 — patchification, 12 blocks, CLS pooling 85,724,928 params · 224² → 768-d

Encoder training

The fine-tune wrapper — two heads that never ship

Fine-tuning is not a third task. MultiTask bolts a step head and an instrument head onto the backbone, trains all three, and then throws the heads away — only backbone.pt is kept, and the cache is re-extracted through it. The second head is free supervision on data already labelled for it.

Task 1 — the current best step model

Spatial embedding → TeCNO → ARST

Three stages, each frozen before the next one trains. That is not an implementation shortcut — it is the published recipe, and it is what makes the cascade trainable on a laptop: no stage ever backpropagates into another. The whole thing is causal, because the challenge permits only online models.

Task 2 — instruments

SANO's causal windowed LSTM

A different problem shape, so a different model: instruments are multi-label — zero, one or two may be visible — so nineteen independent sigmoids, not one argmax. That difference is why per-class thresholds paid here (+0.099 macro) and the same idea failed on steps.

The instrument metric's official column reads the fine-tuned encoder as the worst model tried and both defect-free columns read it as the best by a wide margin. That is the vendored scorer's column-ordering bug, not a trade-off: it fits separate binarizers to truths and predictions, so diverging class sets are compared positionally. It is kept as the headline because the headline is the challenge's by construction — and printed beside two aligned numbers that are not.
